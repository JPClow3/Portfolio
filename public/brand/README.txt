JPCLOW Brand Assets — quick reference (Manual de Identidade Visual v1.0)

Official colors:
- JPCLOW Blue:  #005EFE (accent / focal points only, ~10%)
- JPCLOW Dark:  #1D2026 (structural, strong text, dark surfaces)
- White:        #FFFFFF (light surfaces, text on dark, negative logo)

Support palette:
- Light bg #F7F8FA, light surface #FFFFFF, text secondary #667085, border #DDE1E7
- Dark bg #0E1014, dark surface #171A20

Files (SVG is preferred for web; all scale losslessly):
- logo-primary.svg    Full lockup for light backgrounds (alias of jpclow-logo.svg)
- logo-negative.svg   Full lockup, white on transparent, for dark backgrounds
- icon-primary.svg    JP symbol for favicons, avatars, compact UI
- icon-negative.svg   JP symbol, white on transparent, for dark backgrounds
- jpclow-*.svg        Original filenames kept for backwards compatibility

Rules:
- Never redraw, recolor, gradient-fill, shadow/glow, rotate, or distort the logo.
- Full logo minimum ~160px wide digital; symbol minimum ~24px digital.
- Keep clear space around the mark; no competing text, borders, or buttons inside it.
- Negative version only on dark backgrounds (#1D2026, #0E1014, or similar).
- Source of truth is the official Brand Pack; copies here must not diverge.
