## Overview

Veins of Eridûn was a text-based adventure prototype that explored AI-generated narrative and branching player choices. It is archived and is not presented as an active game or maintained service.

## Explored Features

- **AI-driven narrative** — Real-time story generation experiments
- **Branching paths** — Choices that changed the direction of a run
- **Procedural storytelling** — A different narrative shape on each playthrough
- **Fantasy setting** — Exploration of the mysterious world of Eridûn

## The Experience

The prototype treated the AI as a game master responding to player decisions. Its value now is as an archived exploration of player agency and generated storytelling, not as a current product offering.
